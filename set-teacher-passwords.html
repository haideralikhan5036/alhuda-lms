AL-HUDA ISLAMIC CENTRE
OWNER TOOL — SET PORTAL PASSWORDS

Give each teacher, manager or parent a login password for their secure portal. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is saved or sent anywhere except the LMS server.

Step 1 — Sign in as Owner
Step 2 — Set a portal password
Pick the person's portal role, then enter their identifier exactly as used at login. Password must be at least 10 characters.
Step 3 — Unlock an account or sign out everywhere
If an account is locked after too many failed logins, unlock it here. "Sign out everywhere" ends every session of one account immediately (that person logs in again afterwards).
After setting a password, the person logs in at their own portal (Teacher, Manager or Parent) with that username and password.
